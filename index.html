Information We Collect

  • 📍
    Precise Location (GPS) Your exact geographic location via device GPS to calculate accurate prayer times, Qibla direction, and nearby mosques. Location is only accessed while the app is in use and is never transmitted to our servers or stored externally.
  • 🧭
    Device Sensors (Compass & Accelerometer) We access your device's magnetometer (compass) and accelerometer solely to determine your device orientation and show the correct Qibla direction. This sensor data is processed entirely on your device and is never collected, stored, or transmitted to any server.
  • 🔔
    Notification Preferences We store your prayer notification settings — which prayers to notify for and your preferred Adhan sound — locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device.
  • 📿
    Prayer & Worship Data Information you generate such as Tasbih counts, prayer completion logs, and daily goals are stored exclusively on your device using local storage. We have no access to this data.
  • ⚙️
    App Preferences Settings such as calculation method, Madhab preference, theme selection, and cached location are stored locally on your device only. They are never sent to external servers.
2

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require account creation or collect your name, email, or any personal identifiers
  • We do not transmit your GPS coordinates to our own servers
  • We do not record or store your compass or sensor readings
  • We do not track your prayer habits or worship history on any external server
  • We do not sell your personal information to any third party
3

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to provide the core features of the app:

  • 🕌
    Calculate accurate daily prayer times based on your GPS location using the open-source adhan.js library
  • 🧭
    Determine the precise Qibla direction toward the Kaaba in Makkah using your GPS and compass sensor
  • 📍
    Display nearby mosques using the publicly available OpenStreetMap Overpass API
  • 🔔
    Schedule and deliver local Adhan notifications at prayer times on your device
  • 💾
    Save your personal preferences between app sessions using local device storage
4

Device Permissions

Our app requests the following permissions. You may revoke any permission at any time through your device settings.

Permission Why We Need It
📍Precise Location (GPS) To calculate prayer times, Qibla direction, and find mosques near your exact position
🧭Compass / Motion Sensors To detect device orientation and display the correct Qibla direction
🔔Notifications To deliver Adhan alerts at prayer times as scheduled by you
🌐Internet Access To fetch nearby mosque data from OpenStreetMap and perform reverse geocoding
📳Vibration To provide haptic feedback on the Tasbih counter and when facing the Qibla direction

Revoking location permission will cause prayer times to fall back to your last known cached location. Revoking notification permission will disable Adhan alerts.

5

Third-Party Services

Our app interacts with the following third-party services.

  • Maps
    OpenStreetMap Overpass API

    Used to find nearby mosques based on your location. Your approximate coordinates are sent to retrieve mosque data. OpenStreetMap Privacy Policy →

  • Geocode
    Nominatim (OpenStreetMap)

    Used to convert your GPS coordinates into a readable city name. Only coordinates are sent — no personal identifiers. Results are cached on your device to minimize requests. Nominatim Privacy Policy →

  • On-Device
    adhan.js (Open Source Library)

    An open-source JavaScript library used entirely on your device to calculate prayer times and Qibla bearing. No data is transmitted to any server.

  • Ads
    Google AdMob

    We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect and use certain information about your device to serve relevant ads, including:

    • Advertising ID (Android Ad ID)
    • IP address and approximate location
    • Device information (model, OS version)
    • App usage and interaction data

    You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time:
    Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete Advertising ID

    AdMob's data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy →

Data Storage & Offline Use

  • 📱
    Local OnlyAll core app data is stored locally on your device. We do not maintain servers or databases containing your personal information.
  • ✈️
    Works OfflinePrayer times are cached in local storage and remain accessible without an internet connection. Your Tasbih history, prayer log, and settings are always stored on-device only.
  • 🕌
    Mosque CacheMosque data is cached for 24 hours to reduce API requests and enable offline access to recently loaded results.
  • 🗑️
    UninstallWhen you uninstall the app, all locally stored data is permanently deleted from your device.
7

Data Retention

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you are in full control of retention. We do not maintain any servers or databases containing your personal information. You can clear all app data at any time through your device's app settings under Settings → Apps → Qibla Finder → Clear Data.

8

Data Security

All sensitive operations — including prayer time calculation, Qibla bearing computation, and worship tracking — are performed entirely on your device. Since we do not collect or store your data on external servers, the risk of a data breach affecting your personal information is minimal.

Network requests made to OpenStreetMap services are conducted over secure HTTPS connections. We implement appropriate technical measures to protect any data transmitted to third-party APIs.
